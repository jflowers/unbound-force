Stacked OpenSpec changes inherit the predecessor's Constitution Alignment template, including its principle count. The unbound-force org constitution grew to six principles in v1.4.0 (2026-10-14: Principle VI Deterministic Execution added; Principle V Security by Default already present), and its Compliance Review rule requires the planning-phase Constitution Check gate to verify alignment with ALL active org principles. Changes created after the amendment that copy a predecessor's four- or five-principle assessment become incomplete gate artifacts — re-derive the section from the current constitution version before proposing. Observed in fix-dispatch-verdict-derivation iteration 2 (2026-10-20): its proposal assessed only I-IV while the same-capability predecessor dispatch-finalize-determinism assessed I-V and the contemporaneous unleash-direct-review-dispatch verified all six against v1.4.0 on 2026-10-14.
