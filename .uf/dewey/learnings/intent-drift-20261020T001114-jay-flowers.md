When a design decision reverses an approach announced in a change's proposal (e.g., replacing a planned progress artifact with a checkpoint mechanism), update the proposal in the same change. Stale proposal text contradicting the final design reads as intent drift to reviewers even when the spec delta and tasks are internally consistent — proposal, design, and spec delta must tell one story before merge.
