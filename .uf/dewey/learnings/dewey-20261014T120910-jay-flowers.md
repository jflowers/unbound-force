Dewey compiled articles are always written at tier: draft — by both dewey compile and the agent-driven store_compiled path. The 'validated' tier is only reachable via explicit dewey promote after human review. Spec artifacts and proposals citing Dewey evidence must verify the on-disk frontmatter tier matches the claimed tier before asserting it; calling a draft-tier article 'validated' misrepresents provenance under the Observable Quality principle.
