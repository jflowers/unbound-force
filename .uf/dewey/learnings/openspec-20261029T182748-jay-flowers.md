When a change retires deviation records in a completed sibling change, enumerate the retirement scope by re-route footprint — every fragment describing prose the re-route removes — not by the deviation-class label inherited from the authorizing issue. uf-fix-ci's design D1 deviation inventory lists one entry per prose deviation (three fetch classes, Phase 1.0 availability checks, Phase 1.2 eligibility routing, Phase 2.0 run-id derivation, git invocations, gate scaffolding); a retirement bounded to "the three fetch classes" updated the spec fragments for every affected phase but left the design-side D1 entries for Phase 1.0/1.2 describing prose the re-route removed, their "pinned by the structural contract test" claims falsified by the contract-test rewrite in the same change. Corollary: keep such enumerations in one artifact and reference it from the others — six parallel copies drifted within a single fix iteration (the design's risk section R3 omitted FR-001 after five other copies were extended).
