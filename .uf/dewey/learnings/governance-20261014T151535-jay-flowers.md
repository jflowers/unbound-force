When codifying a governance rule across multiple enforcement layers (org constitution, review convention pack, AGENTS.md behavioral rule, skill), keep the MUST-rule wording identical in every layer. A derived layer that adds a softening qualifier — e.g., AGENTS.md's "where an executable tool is feasible" versus the unqualified MUST in Constitution Principle VI / pack rule DE-001 in the determinism-codification change — creates citable strictness divergence: reviewers loading different layers enforce different thresholds, and the constitution's supremacy clause only resolves the conflict after someone notices it. Cross-layer wording diffing should be an explicit spec-review step for any multi-layer codification change.
