Curation-gate findings stay resolved across review iterations when the human gate decision is recorded with provenance. In the sibling-evidence-agent-guidance review (quick tier), the Curator's iteration-2 HIGH finding — missing in-repo docs, unbound-force/website, and tutorial issues per the documentation gates — was closed by a human deferral recorded directly in the CHANGELOG entry, naming who decided (jflowers), where (the review-council curation gate question tool), when (2026-10-30), and exactly which issues were deferred. On the next iteration the Curator verified the record (CHANGELOG entry present and accurate; issue tracker confirms no such issues were filed) instead of re-filing the finding, letting the review loop converge. Pattern: quality-gate exemptions are human-owned decisions — a reviewer re-asserting a gate against an explicit recorded human decision overrides the authority the gatekeeping framework reserves for the human. The convergent reviewer move is to verify the record's provenance (who/when/where/what) and approve, optionally capturing scope notes for the deferred work as non-blocking advisories.
