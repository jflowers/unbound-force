When recording Constitution Principle VI efficiency evidence, compute growth percentages from the same units as the reported counts. In opsx/unleash-direct-review-dispatch tasks.md 5.4 (2026-10-14), the record "+1,227 words (~+1,600 tokens, ~44% growth; 3625 → 4852 words)" divided the token delta by the word baseline (1600/3625 = 44%) while the reported word counts compute to +33.8%. Mixing a token delta with a word baseline inflates the growth claim in the exact artifact reviewers cite as measurable evidence; the correct figure from the reported words is ~34%.
