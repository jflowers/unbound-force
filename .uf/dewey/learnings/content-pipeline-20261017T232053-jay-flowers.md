When a change pilots a new standard with deferred promotion phases (e.g., command-prose-pilot: pilot in the change, skill+pack promotion in phase 2, corpus rollout in phase 3), blog/tutorial content opportunities and the website-gate obligation attach to the promotion change, not the pilot. The pilot sits below blog significance thresholds (no new agent, command, executed migration, or hero capability), and announcing an unpromoted standard is premature when validation evidence is directional (small-sample A/B with a recorded methodology deviation). But the promotion change that adds a skill or convention pack crosses every threshold at once — per learning documentation-20261014T123134, a convention-pack addition requires a website documentation issue (not exempt) — and the rollout change is the blog moment, with the pilot's measured before/after deltas and comprehension A/B already serving as evidence material. Record a content-pipeline disposition in the pilot's proposal follow-ons (deferred-to-phase-2 blog/tutorial/website items, or a dated maintainer exemption) so the promotion change inherits explicit tracking instead of rediscovering it; under the documentation-gate-20261015T161945 pattern an unrecorded disposition reads as "not considered" even when deferral was intended.
