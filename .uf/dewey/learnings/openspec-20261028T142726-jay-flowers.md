OpenSpec proposal Constitution Alignment sections must assess ALL principles in the active org constitution, not copy a sibling change's section. Since v1.4.0 (2026-10-14) the Unbound Force org constitution has six principles (I Direction, II Quality, III Prudence, IV Transparency, V Security by Default, VI Deterministic Execution); older changes carry four-principle assessments, so copying one silently omits V and VI. Verify the principle count in .specify/memory/constitution.md before writing or copying an alignment section.
