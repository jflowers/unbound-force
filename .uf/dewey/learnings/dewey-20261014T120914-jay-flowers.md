When a spec cites a Dewey knowledge artifact's trust tier as evidence, verify the artifact's frontmatter in the same change. Compiled articles and learnings default to tier: draft; "validated" requires human promotion (dewey promote / the promote MCP tool). Citing a draft-tier artifact as "validated" inflates evidence provenance in governance documents and violates content-pack FA-001 (factual claims verified against actual state).
