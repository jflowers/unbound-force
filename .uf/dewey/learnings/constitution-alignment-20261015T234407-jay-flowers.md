Constitution amendments invalidate the principle enumeration in spec templates. After the org constitution moved 1.3.1 → 1.4.0 (adding Principle VI, Deterministic Execution, on 2026-10-14), proposals created afterward still patterned their Constitution Alignment sections on the four-principle shape (I–IV only), even when the change directly exercised the new principle via the determinism gate. Proposals and verification tasks that enumerate principles (Constitution Alignment sections, tasks.md constitution-verification tasks) must be re-baselined against the current constitution version at creation time — the Compliance Review rule requires the planning-phase check to cover ALL active principles, and a newly added principle is often the one a change most directly exercises.
