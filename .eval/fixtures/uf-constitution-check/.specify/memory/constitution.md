## Principles

### 1. Test-Driven Confidence

Every change must be accompanied by tests that verify its correctness.
Untested code is unfinished code. Tests are not optional — they are the
primary evidence that the system works as intended.

### 2. Transparent Decision Making

All architectural and design decisions must be documented with rationale.
When trade-offs are made, the alternatives considered and reasons for
rejection must be recorded alongside the chosen approach.

### 3. Incremental Delivery

Work is delivered in small, reviewable increments. Each increment must
be independently deployable and must not break existing functionality.
Large changes are decomposed into a sequence of safe steps.
